# Programs by Region — Virginia Heat Vulnerability Interventions

> All programs below are **real, active programs** as of 2025–2026.
> Contact numbers are provided where publicly listed.
> **If a city is not listed here, only recommend statewide programs (see Statewide section).**

## STATEWIDE (Available in All Virginia Jurisdictions)

### LIHEAP — Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program
- **What:** One-time annual payment toward electric or gas bill; emergency repair for broken AC/heating equipment
- **Funded by:** Federal (DOE) + Commonwealth of Virginia
- **Administered by:** Local community action agencies (one per county/city)
- **Eligibility:** Income ≤ 150% Federal Poverty Level (2025: ~$22,050 for household of 1)
- **How to apply:** Call **2-1-1 Virginia** (dial 211) or find your local CAA at [211virginia.org](https://211virginia.org)
- **Finn framework tie:** #5 (affordable cooling) and #4 (housing)

### Virginia Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP)
- **What:** Free in-home energy upgrades: insulation, air sealing, AC repair/replacement, heat pump installation
- **Administered by:** Local community action agencies + DVA contractors
- **Eligibility:** Income ≤ 60% AFI (Area Fannie Mae Income); priority for elderly, disabled, families with children under 6
- **How to apply:** Call **2-1-1 Virginia** or your local CAA
- **Finn framework tie:** #4 (improve rental housing) and #5 (affordable cooling)

### Dominion Energy Virginia — Assistance Programs
- **What:**
  - **Customer Assistance Program:** Bill payment plans, arrears forgiveness
  - **Weatherization:** Free AC units, insulation, air sealing (see WAP above)
  - **Energy Conservation Program:** Free efficient light bulbs, AC filters, thermostat upgrades
- **Eligibility:** Dominion Energy Virginia residential customer; income guidelines vary by program
- **How to apply:** Call **1-888-847-2262** or visit [dominionenergy.com/assistance](https://www.dominionenergy.com/residential)
- **Finn framework tie:** #5 (affordable cooling)

### 2-1-1 Virginia
- **What:** Free, confidential 24/7 referral to 2,000+ social services including cooling centers, utility assistance, and housing
- **How to access:** Dial **2-1-1** or visit **211virginia.org**
- **Finn framework tie:** All 5 (gateway to every other program)

### Virginia Statewide Cooling Centers (Heat Warning Protocol)
- **What:** When VMA issues a Heat Advisory or Heat Warning, local jurisdictions are required to open cooling centers
- **Locations:** Senior centers, libraries, community centers — vary by city
- **Hours during Heat Warning:** Typically 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- **Eligibility:** Open to all residents
- **How to find:** Call your city's 311 line or check city website when a heat warning is active
- **Finn framework tie:** #5 (safe and affordable cooling)

## RICHMOND METRO (Richmond City, Henrico County, Chesterfield County)

**Regional utility:** Dominion Energy Virginia (all three jurisdictions)
**Regional CAA (LIHEAP/WAP admin):** Peninsula Family Services — Richmond Metro
- **What:** Administers LIHEAP, WAP weatherization, cooling assistance, and emergency assistance in Richmond City, Henrico, and Chesterfield
- **How to apply:** Call **800-394-0308** or visit [pfsv.org](https://pfsv.org)
- **Finn framework tie:** #4 (housing) and #5 (affordable cooling)

### City of Richmond — Urban Forestry / Canopy Equity
- **What:** Richmond's Department of Public Works runs a tree program including street tree planting and removal permits; Richmond has published a tree canopy equity plan targeting low-canopy neighborhoods
- **Key actions the city commits to:** protect mature street trees, expand canopy in the hottest, lowest-income neighborhoods
- **How to engage:** Contact the **City of Richmond, Department of Public Works — Forestry/Urban Forestry** via [richmondgov.com](https://www.richmondgov.com) or call **804-646-4000** (city main line)
- **Finn framework tie:** #1 (protect mature canopy) and #2 (plant where heat is greatest)

### City of Richmond — Tree Planting (Free Trees)
- **What:** Annual free tree giveaways and permit-based street tree planting; residents can request trees through city channels
- **How to apply:** Call **804-646-4000** or ask 311 (dial 311 when in the city) for the current free tree program season
- **Finn framework tie:** #2 (plant and maintain trees)

### Peninsula Family Services — Weatherization & Cooling
- **What:** Free in-home weatherization (insulation, air sealing, AC repair/replacement, heat pumps) + cooling assistance for eligible households in the Richmond Metro
- **Eligibility:** Income ≤ 60% AFI; priority for elderly, disabled, families with young children; tenants and homeowners both eligible
- **How to apply:** Call **800-394-0308** or dial **2-1-1**
- **Finn framework tie:** #4 (improve rental housing) and #5 (affordable cooling)

### Richmond Cooling Centers
- **What:** During heat warnings, Richmond opens cooling centers at public libraries, senior centers, and community centers
- **How to find:** Call **804-646-4000** or dial **311** during a Heat Advisory/Heat Warning; also check [richmondgov.com](https://www.richmondgov.com)
- **Finn framework tie:** #5 (safe and affordable cooling)

## HAMPTON ROADS (Chesapeake, Hampton, Newport News, Norfolk, Poquoson, Portsmouth, Suffolk, Virginia Beach, York)

> **Regional note:** Hampton Roads has multiple community action agencies — one or more per jurisdiction. The single most reliable route for LIHEAP, weatherization, and cooling assistance in ANY Hampton Roads city/county is: **dial 2-1-1** (Virginia) and the operator routes you to the correct local agency. Utilities: Dominion Energy Virginia (most cities) and **VRE (Virginia Renewable Energy)** in parts of the region — call the utility on the customer's bill for their own assistance programs.

### City of Chesapeake — Urban Forestry & Free Trees
- **What:** Chesapeake Department of Public Works runs a street tree program, annual free tree giveaways, and a tree removal permit process that protects established canopy
- **How to engage:** Visit [chesapeake.gov](https://www.chesapeake.gov) (Public Works → Trees) or call **757-436-6000** (city main line)
- **Finn framework tie:** #1 (protect mature canopy) and #2 (plant where heat is greatest)

### City of Hampton — Urban Forestry
- **What:** Hampton Public Works manages street trees, planting requests, and a tree removal permit program
- **How to engage:** Visit [hamptonva.gov](https://hamptonva.gov) or call **757-720-2000** (city main line)
- **Finn framework tie:** #1 (protect mature canopy) and #2 (plant and maintain trees)

### City of Hampton — Cooling & Weatherization (via local CAA)
- **What:** Hampton households qualify for LIHEAP, WAP weatherization, and cooling assistance administered by the local community action agency (Peninsula Family Services — Hampton/New Kent/Poquoson/York area)
- **How to apply:** Call **800-394-0308** (Peninsula Family Services) or dial **2-1-1**
- **Finn framework tie:** #4 (improve rental housing) and #5 (affordable cooling)

### City of Newport News — Trees & Canopy
- **What:** Newport News Public Works maintains the street tree program and tree removal permits; the **Newport News Green Foundation** runs free tree giveaways and a "tree of the month" program, and co-developed the heat-inequality findings in `key_findings.md`
- **How to engage:**
  - Trees/permits: [newportnews.gov](https://www.newportnews.gov) (Public Works) or call **757-835-4000** (city main line)
  - Free trees & planting: **Newport News Green Foundation**, call **757-864-4234** or [newportnewsgreenfoundation.org](https://www.newportnewsgreenfoundation.org)
- **Finn framework tie:** #1 (protect mature canopy) and #2 (plant where heat is greatest)

### City of Newport News — Cooling & Weatherization
- **What:** Newport News households qualify for LIHEAP, WAP weatherization (insulation, AC repair/replacement, heat pumps), and emergency cooling assistance; administration is handled by the local community action agency (Peninsula Family Services for this area)
- **Eligibility:** LIHEAP income ≤ 150% FPL; WAP income ≤ 60% AFI; tenants and homeowners both eligible
- **How to apply:** Call **800-394-0308** (Peninsula Family Services) or dial **2-1-1**
- **Finn framework tie:** #4 (improve rental housing) and #5 (affordable cooling)

### City of Newport News — Cooling Centers
- **What:** During heat warnings, Newport News opens cooling centers at public libraries, senior centers, and recreation centers
- **How to find:** Call **757-835-4000** (city main line) or check [newportnews.gov](https://www.newportnews.gov) during a Heat Advisory/Heat Warning
- **Finn framework tie:** #5 (safe and affordable cooling)

### City of Norfolk — Urban Forestry
- **What:** Norfolk's Department of Public Works runs a street tree planting program, annual free tree events, and a tree removal permit program; Norfolk has one of the region's longest urban forestry histories
- **How to engage:** Visit [norfolk.gov](https://www.norfolk.gov) (Public Works → Urban Forestry) or call **757-727-6000** (city main line)
- **Finn framework tie:** #1 (protect mature canopy) and #2 (plant and maintain trees)

### City of Norfolk — Cooling & Weatherization
- **What:** Norfolk households qualify for LIHEAP, WAP weatherization, and cooling assistance administered by the local community action agency (Norfolk's CAA: Peninsula Family Services serves the Hampton Roads peninsula, including Norfolk, or the agency reached via 2-1-1)
- **How to apply:** Call **800-394-0308** (Peninsula Family Services) or dial **2-1-1** for routing to the correct local agency
- **Finn framework tie:** #4 (improve rental housing) and #5 (affordable cooling)

### City of Poquoson
- **What:** Poquoson households qualify for LIHEAP, WAP weatherization, and cooling assistance (administered via the Peninsula area CAA — Peninsula Family Services); tree and canopy requests go through Poquoson Public Works
- **How to apply:** Call **800-394-0308** or dial **2-1-1**; city main line **757-839-3000**
- **Finn framework tie:** #4 (housing) and #5 (affordable cooling)

### City of Portsmouth
- **What:** Portsmouth has a Department of Public Works street tree program with a removal permit process; Portsmouth residents also qualify for LIHEAP, WAP weatherization, and cooling assistance through the regional community action agency (2-1-1 routes correctly)

- **Finn framework tie:** #1/#2 (trees) and #4/#5 (housing and cooling)

### City of Suffolk
- **What:** Suffolk Public Works handles street tree planting and permits; Suffolk residents qualify for LIHEAP and WAP weatherization via the local CAA (2-1-1 routes correctly)
- **How to engage:** [suffolkva.gov](https://www.suffolkva.gov) (Public Works); assistance via **2-1-1** or **800-394-0308**
- **Finn framework tie:** #1/#2 (trees) and #4/#5 (housing and cooling)

### City of Virginia Beach — Urban Forestry
- **What:** Virginia Beach's Urban Forestry Division (Dept. of Public Works) operates one of Virginia's largest street tree programs, including canopy assessments targeting low-canopy neighborhoods, free tree giveaways, and removal permits
- **How to engage:** Visit [vabeach.gov](https://vabeach.gov) (Urban Forestry) or call **757-385-5000** (city main line); tree requests also via **311**

- **How to apply:** Call **800-394-0308** (Peninsula Family Services) or dial **2-1-1**

### City of Virginia Beach — Cooling Centers & 311
- **What:** Virginia Beach 311 is the primary line during heat events for cooling center locations, utility assistance referrals, and tree-related requests
- **How to access:** Dial **311** (in city) or **757-436-3131**
- **Finn framework tie:** #5 (safe and affordable cooling)

### York County
